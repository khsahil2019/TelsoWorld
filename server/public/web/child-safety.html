Child Safety Standards & CSAE Prevention Policy

Application: Telso World (Package: com.telsoworld.app)
Published Online: https://telsoworld.com/child-safety
Effective Date: November 2026

1. Zero-Tolerance Policy for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Strict Zero-Tolerance: Telso World maintains an absolute zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE), child grooming, predatory solicitation, or any behavior that threatens the safety or well-being of minors.

We are deeply committed to creating a safe and trustworthy digital environment. Any account that uploads, shares, stores, solicits, links to, or attempts to distribute content depicting or facilitating child exploitation will be immediately and permanently banned, and the incident will be reported directly to global child safety authorities and law enforcement.

2. Prohibited Conduct Regarding Minors

The following activities are strictly forbidden on Telso World under all circumstances:

  • Uploading, transmitting, publishing, or sharing any imagery, videos, or animations depicting child sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Child grooming, predatory communications, sexual solicitation of minors, or attempting to obtain private contact information from children.
  • Depicting, promoting, or normalizing child physical abuse, self-harm, trafficking, or endangerment.
  • Using direct messaging or comments to harass, stalk, intimidate, or exploit minors.

3. Reporting Mechanisms & Immediate Action

We provide accessible and rapid reporting channels for all users to report potential child safety violations:

How to Report Violations:

1. In-App Reporting (Fastest Response):

  • On Reels & Photos: Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) on the video or photo card and select "Report" > "Child Safety / Inappropriate Content".
  • On User Profiles: Tap the options menu on any user's profile and choose "Report User" or tap "Block" to instantly sever all communications.
  • In Direct Chat: Tap and hold any message or tap the chat header to report the conversation directly to our moderation team.

2. Dedicated Child Safety Email:

You can submit urgent child safety concerns directly to our specialized safety team at:
👉 safety@telsoworld.com
(All child safety reports receive immediate priority review 24/7).

4. Moderation, Law Enforcement & NCMEC Cooperation

To uphold these standards, Telso World utilizes multi-layered detection and enforcement workflows:

  • Automated & Manual Review: User reports flagging child safety violations are prioritized ahead of all standard moderation queues.
  • Immediate Account Termination: Confirmed violators are permanently barred with device, IP, and account blacklisting.
  • Mandatory Reporting to NCMEC: In accordance with international obligations and Google Play safety standards, any verified instances of CSAM/CSAE are reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant national law enforcement agencies.
  • Evidence Preservation: Relevant server logs, IP records, and file metadata are securely preserved solely for lawful transmission to investigating authorities.

5. Age Requirements & Parental Rights

Telso World is intended strictly for individuals aged 13 years and older (or the minimum legal age for social media participation in your country).

We do not permit children under 13 to create accounts. If a parent, legal guardian, or community member believes an underage account exists, please alert us at safety@telsoworld.com and the account will be promptly reviewed and terminated.

6. Designated Safety Point of Contact

In compliance with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy for social applications, the designated Point of Contact for child safety matters is:

  • Entity: Telso World Trust & Safety Team
  • Child Safety Email: safety@telsoworld.com
  • General Support: support@telsoworld.com
  • Application: Telso World (Package ID: com.telsoworld.app)
