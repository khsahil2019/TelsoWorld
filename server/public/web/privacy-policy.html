1. Introduction

Welcome to Telso World ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, process, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (Telso World, package com.telsoworld.app) and related online services available at https://telsoworld.com.

By downloading, installing, registering for, or using Telso World, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not access or use our services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and enhance our social platform:

A. Information You Provide Directly to Us

  • Account Registration Details: When you sign up, we collect your name, username, email address, mobile phone number, and password (securely hashed with industry-standard bcrypt encryption).
  • Profile Information: Profile avatar photo, bio/description, and custom social links you choose to add.
  • User-Generated Content (UGC): Short videos (Reels), photos, captions, audio titles, comments, likes, and 24-hour disappearing Stories uploaded through the app.
  • Direct Communications: 1-on-1 direct chat messages, replies, and requests exchanged between users.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Hardware Data: Operating system version, device model, screen resolution, and unique device identifiers to deliver optimal video rendering.
  • Log & Usage Information: Video view counts, likes count, share counts, time stamps of logins, and performance crash logs.
  • Network Information: IP address and connection type (Wi-Fi, 4G, 5G) used to serve video content smoothly.

C. Device Permissions We Request

Permission Technical Name Purpose of Use
Camera android.permission.CAMERA Allows you to capture new video reels, take photos, and record 24-hour stories directly in the app.
Microphone android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO Allows you to record audio and sound tracks when capturing video reels and stories.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Delivering Core Platform Services: Authenticating user accounts, streaming short videos, displaying user photos, rendering 24-hour stories, and delivering direct chat messages.
  • Community & Social Interactions: Enabling followers, likes, comments, and discovery through the Explore search grid.
  • Platform Security & Abuse Prevention: Detecting fraudulent accounts, spam, abusive content, and enforcing our Community Guidelines. Users have the ability to block abusive users and report content.
  • Service Improvement: Analyzing video playback performance, crash diagnostics, and ensuring optimal video encoding across Android devices.
Strict Policy on User Data: We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data or user-generated content to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

4. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We only share information under the following limited circumstances:

  • Public Content: Content you upload publicly (reels, public photos, public comments, username, and bio) is visible to other users of Telso World.
  • Private Messages: Direct messages are strictly transmitted between the sender and recipient and are not publicly visible.
  • Trusted Service Providers: Cloud hosting, video delivery CDN networks, and database infrastructure providers who operate under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by applicable laws, court orders, or governmental regulations to protect the safety, rights, and property of Telso World and our community.

5. Data Security & Encryption

We employ robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between your device and our servers are encrypted using modern HTTPS / Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3).
  • Password Protection: Passwords are never stored in plaintext; they are hashed with salted cryptographic algorithms (bcrypt).
  • Access Controls: Administrative access to backend database systems is restricted strictly to authorized engineering personnel using multi-factor authentication.

6. Account & Data Deletion Policy

In full compliance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion policies, you have complete control over your account and personal data. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion:

Method 1: Directly inside the Telso World Mobile App

  1. Open the Telso World app and navigate to your Profile tab.
  2. Tap the Menu / Settings icon in the top right corner.
  3. Select Account Settings > Delete Account.
  4. Confirm deletion. Your account, profile details, uploaded reels, photos, stories, and messages will be permanently purged.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request Form

You can submit a deletion request online without opening the app at:
👉 https://telsoworld.com/delete-account

Method 3: Direct Email Request

Send an email from your registered email address to support@telsoworld.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Requests are processed within 48 to 72 business hours.

Data Removed Upon Deletion:

  • Profile information (Username, email, phone number, avatar, bio).
  • All uploaded video reels, photos, captions, and comments.
  • All 24-hour stories and highlights.
  • Direct chat history and message records.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Telso World is intended for users aged 13 and older (or the minimum legal age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13.

If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child under 13 without verified parental consent, we take immediate steps to delete that information from our servers and terminate the associated account. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided us with personal information can contact us at support@telsoworld.com.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Edit and update inaccurate profile information directly in the app.
  • Right to Erasure: Request permanent deletion of your data as outlined in Section 6.
  • Right to Restrict or Object: Control privacy options, including blocking users and managing who can view your content.
