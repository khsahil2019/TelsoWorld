Delete Your Telso World Account

In accordance with Google Play's User Data Policy, users of Telso World (com.telsoworld.app) can request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data.

What data will be deleted?

Upon processing your deletion request, the following data will be permanently wiped:

  • User profile (Name, username, email, phone number, bio, avatar)
  • All published video reels, photos, captions, and comments
  • All uploaded 24-hour stories and highlights
  • Direct chat message history
  • Followers and following connections

Online Deletion Request Form

✅ Your deletion request has been received. Our team will verify and permanently delete your account within 48 to 72 hours.
You can also delete your account instantly inside the Telso World app: Profile > Settings > Account > Delete Account, or email us directly at support@telsoworld.com.
